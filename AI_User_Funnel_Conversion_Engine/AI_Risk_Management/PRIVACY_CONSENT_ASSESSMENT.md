# Privacy & Consent Assessment

## Data Principle
Use the minimum behavioral data required to support onboarding, activation, and retention assistance.

## Personalization Off
If disabled:
- no predictive intervention
- no AI-targeted lifecycle messaging
- static onboarding remains available
- aggregate analytics may continue only according to applicable settings

## Prohibited Data
Protected traits
precise geolocation
health data
political views
biometrics
financial hardship signals
raw payment credentials
private communications

## Deletion
Eligible user-linked feature records and future training snapshots must honor deletion workflows.

## Review Trigger
Any new third-party data, cross-device identity linking, retention expansion, or sensitive-category product use requires fresh review.

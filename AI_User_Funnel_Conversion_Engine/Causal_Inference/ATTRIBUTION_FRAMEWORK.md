# Funnel Attribution Framework

## Levels of Evidence
### Level 1 — Descriptive
Users exposed to intervention converted more often.
Not causal.

### Level 2 — Adjusted Observational
Regression / matching attempts to control confounding.
Still weaker than randomized evidence.

### Level 3 — Randomized Experiment
Preferred for product launch decisions.

### Level 4 — Incrementality / Uplift
Estimates which users benefit because of treatment.

## Reporting Language
Use:
- observed lift
- experiment-estimated lift
- projected financial impact
- simulated portfolio outcome

Do not label projections as realized revenue.

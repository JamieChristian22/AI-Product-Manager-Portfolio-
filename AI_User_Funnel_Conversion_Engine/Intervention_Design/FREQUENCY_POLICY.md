# Cross-Channel Frequency Policy

## High-Salience In-Product
Max 1 per 24 hours.

## Lifecycle Email
Max 3 per 7 days for AI-targeted growth interventions.

## Push / Mobile
Max 2 per 7 days.

## Cross-Channel
Max 4 total AI-targeted interventions per 7-day rolling window.

## Suppression
- after two dismissals: 7-day suppression
- after complaint: 30-day suppression pending review
- after opt-out: indefinite suppression unless user opts back in

Frequency policy is enforced through a shared intervention ledger.

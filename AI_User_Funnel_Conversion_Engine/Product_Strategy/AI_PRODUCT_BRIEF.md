# AI Product Brief

## Problem
Traditional funnel analytics explain where users drop, but not which users are likely to struggle next or which intervention is most likely to help.

## Product Hypothesis
If the product predicts activation, churn, and conversion risk and uses those predictions to select contextually relevant interventions, then more users will reach product value faster and remain engaged longer.

## Primary Users
- Product managers
- Growth teams
- Lifecycle teams
- Product analysts
- Data scientists
- Customer education teams

## Non-Goals
- individualized pricing
- blocking cancellation
- eligibility decisions
- sensitive profiling
- replacing experimentation with model scores
- maximizing clicks independent of retention

## North Star
Engagement-Adjusted Revenue (EAR), with mandatory retention, trust, and autonomy guardrails.

## Key Tradeoff
Aggressive intervention can improve short-term conversion while reducing long-term trust. The system explicitly treats opt-outs, complaints, dismissals, and retention decline as negative product outcomes.

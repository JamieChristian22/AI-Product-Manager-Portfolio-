# AI Risk Taxonomy

## 1. Data Risk
Missingness, stale features, schema drift, consent mismatch, leakage, biased historical exposure.

## 2. Model Risk
Overfitting, calibration error, drift, poor cold-start behavior, unstable retraining.

## 3. User Risk
Filter bubbles, manipulation, loss of autonomy, confusing explanations, irrelevant personalization.

## 4. Fairness Risk
Proxy discrimination, unequal ranking quality, underexposure of minority catalogs/cohorts.

## 5. Privacy Risk
Sensitive inference, PII leakage, excessive retention, consent bypass, cross-context profiling.

## 6. Security Risk
Prompt injection, poisoning, model extraction, metadata manipulation, supply-chain compromise.

## 7. Business Risk
CTR optimization at expense of revenue/retention, merchant concentration, inventory mismatch.

## 8. Operational Risk
Latency, outages, fallback failure, untracked model versions, poor incident response.

## 9. Governance Risk
Missing ownership, undocumented changes, unapproved feature additions, weak auditability.

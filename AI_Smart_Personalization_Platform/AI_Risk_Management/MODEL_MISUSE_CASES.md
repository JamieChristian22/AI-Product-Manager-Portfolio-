# Model Misuse Cases

| Misuse | Risk | Prevention |
|---|---|---|
| Use propensity score to deny offers | unfair eligibility decision | policy prohibits eligibility use |
| Use behavioral segment as identity label | stereotyping | labels only for internal product strategy |
| Infer health/political status from browsing | sensitive profiling | feature and inference prohibition |
| Maximize clicks regardless of complaints | manipulation | EAR + negative-feedback guardrail |
| Show personalized content after opt-out | consent violation | hard policy gate |
| Use GenAI to fabricate benefits | deception | grounded generation |
| Override diversity for paid placement without disclosure | unfair exposure | policy separation and logging |

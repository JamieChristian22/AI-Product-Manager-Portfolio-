# Experiment Sample Size & Power Example

## Example Assumptions
Baseline conversion = 3.8%
Minimum detectable relative lift = 7%
Absolute MDE = 0.266 percentage points
Alpha = 0.05
Power = 0.80
Two-sided test
Traffic split = 50/50

## Portfolio Planning Estimate
A test would require approximately tens of thousands of users per arm depending on variance, repeat exposure, and exact statistical method. Final production sizing would be computed from observed baseline variance rather than reused blindly.

## Product Rule
Do not launch based on underpowered positive trends. If traffic is insufficient, extend duration or widen eligible population without changing the pre-registered metric.

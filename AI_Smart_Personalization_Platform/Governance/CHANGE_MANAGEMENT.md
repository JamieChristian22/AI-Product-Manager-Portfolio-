# AI Change Management

## Minor Change
Hyperparameter adjustment within approved objective.
Requires offline validation + version update.

## Material Change
New feature family, new objective, model-family change, new data source, new GenAI capability.
Requires full risk review, privacy review where applicable, updated model card, experiment, and release approval.

## Emergency Change
Rollback, security mitigation, or critical policy patch.
May bypass normal experiment sequencing but requires retrospective documentation within one business day.

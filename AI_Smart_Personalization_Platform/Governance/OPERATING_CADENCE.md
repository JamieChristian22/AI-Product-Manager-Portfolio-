# AI Product Operating Cadence

## Daily
- automated quality/drift monitoring
- latency/error review
- critical incident triage

## Weekly
- model-health review
- experiment readout
- user-feedback review
- catalog exposure review

## Biweekly
- challenger-model evaluation
- scheduled retraining review
- feature-quality review

## Monthly
- AI risk register review
- privacy/fairness checkpoint
- product KPI and long-term retention review
- stakeholder governance review

## Quarterly
- model-purpose reassessment
- taxonomy and policy review
- red-team refresh
- retirement candidates

# Model Lifecycle Policy

## Stages
1. Ideation
2. Data feasibility
3. Prototype
4. Offline validation
5. Risk review
6. Shadow test
7. A/B test
8. Staged production
9. Monitoring
10. Retraining
11. Retirement

## Gate Requirements
Each stage requires documented owner, inputs, outputs, risks, decision, and evidence.

## Retirement Triggers
- sustained quality degradation,
- unacceptable fairness/privacy issue,
- obsolete business objective,
- replacement by superior model,
- inability to maintain feature quality,
- repeated incidents.
